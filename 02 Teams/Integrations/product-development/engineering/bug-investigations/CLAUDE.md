# bug-investigations

## What is in this folder

Structured write-ups of past bugs investigated by the Integrations squad.
Format follows bug-investigation-guide.md.

## When to read

Before starting any new bug investigation, check here for similar past issues.
Cross-referencing past investigations saves hours of duplicate diagnosis.

## File naming

YYYY-MM-DD-[ticket-key]-[short-description].md
Example: 2026-05-10-API-123-oauth-token-expiry.md
