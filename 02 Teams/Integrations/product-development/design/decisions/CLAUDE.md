# decisions

## What is in this folder

Closed design decisions with context and rationale.
Designers populate this organically as decisions are made.

## When to read

When query involves a past design decision, rationale, or design direction.

## File naming

YYYY-MM-DD-[decision-topic].md

## Format

Each decision doc should include:
- What was decided
- Why (context and constraints)
- Alternatives considered
- Who made the decision and when
