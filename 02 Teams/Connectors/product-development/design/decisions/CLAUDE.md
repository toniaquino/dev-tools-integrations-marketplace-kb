# decisions

## What is in this folder

Closed design decisions with context and rationale.
One file per decision, named: YYYY-MM-DD-[decision-short-title].md

Designers populate this organically as decisions are made.
No content is seeded here automatically.

## When to read

Read files here when the query involves a past design decision or rationale
for why the product looks or works a certain way.

## Format

Each decision file should contain:
- Decision: what was decided
- Context: why this decision was needed
- Options considered: what alternatives were evaluated
- Rationale: why this option was chosen
- Outcome: what shipped as a result
