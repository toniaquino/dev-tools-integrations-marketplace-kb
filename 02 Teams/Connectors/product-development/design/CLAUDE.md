# design

## What is in this folder

- figma-index.md: links to all active Figma files by initiative
- research/: Dovetail study summaries and synthesis
- decisions/: closed design decisions with context and rationale

## When to read

- figma-index.md: when you need to find a specific Figma file
- research/: when query involves user research, usability findings, or
  synthesis from Dovetail studies
- decisions/: when query involves a past design decision or rationale

## Research format

All Dovetail research summaries follow the format in
.claude/writing-guides/customer-call-guide.md (adapted for research sessions).
