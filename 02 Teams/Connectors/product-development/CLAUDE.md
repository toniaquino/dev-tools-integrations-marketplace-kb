# product-development

## What is in this folder

Everything the Connectors squad produces and references for product work.
Organized by function: product (PRDs, customers, strategy), engineering, design, analytics.

## When to read subfolders

- Feature-specific query: check product/feature-index.yaml first
- Customer context: product/customers/
- Active specs: product/PRDs/integrations-marketplace/
- Bug or architecture: engineering/
- Design decisions or research: design/
- Analytics: PENDING -- do not read yet
