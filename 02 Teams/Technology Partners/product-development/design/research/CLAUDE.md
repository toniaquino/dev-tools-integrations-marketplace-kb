# research

## What is in this folder

Dovetail research study summaries and synthesis documents.
One file per study, named: YYYY-MM-DD-[study-name]-summary.md

## When to read

Read when a query involves user research, usability findings, participant feedback,
or synthesis from past Dovetail studies related to Integrations Marketplace.

## Format

All research summaries use the customer-call-guide.md format adapted for research:
see .claude/writing-guides/customer-call-guide.md
