## Why this partner matters to this squad

Raspberry.ai is a partner-built, free, fully self-serve integration that works today on the
partner's side but has no Bynder Marketplace listing and no certification. That makes it a
clean test case for two Integrations Marketplace workstreams this squad owns (both named in
`../../feature-index.yaml`):

- **Partner Onboarding Process** — the whole path is visible in one partner, and the friction is
  legible: inbound first contact 2026-03-02, agreement sent 2026-03-05 and executed 2026-05-08,
  yet the partner had to ask "how do we publish our integration onto Bynder's marketplace?" on
  2026-06-17, six weeks after signing. Bynder-side tracking only appeared 2026-08-19 (MP-269 +
  INTEGRATE article), and the Technical Validation session still is not confirmed as held. Note
  the ~8–9 week legal stretch is contracting time, not the manual technical-validation time the
  -50% onboarding target in `../../strategy/marketplace-strategy.md` is measured against;
  whether either belongs in the same baseline is an open question.
- **Marketplace Documentation Migration** — the INTEGRATE article was created 2026-08-19 as a
  deliberate skeleton with `TBD`s outstanding, i.e. an example of the per-partner page pattern
  being used for a partner whose content is not yet validated.

The live-integration-without-a-listing gap is the thing to watch: on 2026-08-18 sales were
told they could tell a customer the integration is live, and on 2026-08-19 MP-269 was opened
as Under Development and the INTEGRATE article published with a "do not use for customer-facing
commitments until the state is Available" banner. See the canonical entry's "Current state" →
"Framing risk to watch".
