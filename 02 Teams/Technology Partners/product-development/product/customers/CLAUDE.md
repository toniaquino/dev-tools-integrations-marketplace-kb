# customers

## What is in this folder

One subfolder per key customer account or segment.
Each contains: a summary.md (key contacts, segment, account context)
and a calls/ subfolder with individual Gong call summaries.

or in-certification partners as the primary accounts here.

## When to read

- summary.md: for partner context, key contacts, or relationship history
- calls/: for specific call content or meeting notes
- Do not load all call summaries for a general partner query --
  read summary.md first. Go into calls/ only if summary does not have what you need.
