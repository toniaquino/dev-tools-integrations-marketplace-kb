## Why this partner matters to this squad

Corsearch is the furthest-advanced partner tracked in this domain and the only one to have
passed certification, so it is the reference path for what "done" looks like. It is also the
slowest — about eleven months from first contact to signature, against Raspberry.ai's nine
weeks and CloudCannon's eight days — which makes it the most informative case for the two
Integrations Marketplace workstreams this squad owns (both named in `../../feature-index.yaml`):

- **Partner Onboarding Process** — the single most reusable finding in this domain sits here.
  Section 1.1 of the Technology Partnership Agreement template said the *Partner* would
  "design, develop, create… and provide" the API. That is backwards for every partner-built
  integration, and it cost roughly ten weeks (2026-04-17 → 2026-06-24) with Bynder legal as the
  bottleneck for most of it. Bill Mostyn said on 2026-05-12 "I'll update in template" —
  **confirm that landed, because CloudCannon and Raspberry.ai sign the same paper.** A template
  defect that recurs across every partner is worth more to the onboarding baseline than any
  single partner's cycle time.
- **Marketplace Documentation Migration** — the 2026-08-13 Doc Migration wave rewrote both
  MP-254's description and the INTEGRATE article from the partner's intake and marketing forms.
  The result is the most complete partner article in the domain, with very few `TBC`s. It is
  also the clearest example of the migration introducing a defect: the article's changelog
  claims "August 2026 — Integration released" with no author, while the integration is
  Awaiting Release and unlisted.

Three things to watch. First, **partner-side continuity**: ownership has changed three times in
twelve months (Sedara → Standing → Rodenheiser → Markovic/Gagliano), and each handover cost
weeks. Pietro Gagliano is the only durable counterpart — treat him as the relationship, not the
roster. Second, **an unresolved commercial tail**: the Referral Agreement has been open since
October 2025, Corsearch cannot locate the executed NDA, and the 10% first-year ARR commission
rests on a Zoom AI meeting recap rather than signed paper. Third, **the largest Crossbeam
overlap in the file** — 900+ Bynder customers are Corsearch prospects, ~8,200 joint prospects —
against an unanswered question from 2026-04-30 about whether Bynder's co-sell process is
Crossbeam-driven.

Note also that this is the only **co-sell** partnership in this file rather than only an
integration, and the only partner whose product was already appearing in Bynder accounts as a
third-party vendor before the partnership existed.
