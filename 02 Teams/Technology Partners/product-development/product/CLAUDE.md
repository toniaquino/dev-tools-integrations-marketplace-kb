# product

## What is in this folder

All product artifacts: PRDs, customer context, competitive research, strategy, launches.

## Feature index

ALWAYS check feature-index.yaml before reading subfolders for feature-specific queries.
It maps every feature to its PRDs, RFCs, customer context, and metrics in one lookup.

## When to read subfolders

| Subfolder | Read when |
|---|---|
| PRDs/ | Query involves a specific feature spec or requirements |
| customers/ | Query involves a customer, account, or call summary |
| competitive-research/ | Query involves a competitor or market comparison |
| strategy/ | Query involves roadmap, vision, or business context |
| launches/ | Query involves a launch, GTM, or release |
| processes/ | Query involves team process or operational guidance |
