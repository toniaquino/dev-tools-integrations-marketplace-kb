## Standardize the marketplace listing intake process
**Date:** 2026-06-08
**Owner:** Bas van Reeuwijk
**Status:** Decided
**Context:** Partner onboarding to the marketplace had been inconsistent.
**Decision:** Every new listing follows a showcase meeting plus a Content Workflow (GatherContent) form per partner, after which the partner enters the release-cycle communications.
**Implications:** Predictable, repeatable intake; partners are gated on providing their own listing content before publication.
**Source:** Slack, 2026-06-08.

## Certification as a prerequisite for selling through the marketplace
**Date:** 2025-07-28
**Owner:** Bas van Reeuwijk
**Status:** Decided
**Context:** Exploring marketplace monetization via a pay-to-play / transaction-fee model (AWS-Marketplace style).
**Decision:** Partner certification will be required in order to sell through the marketplace.
**Implications:** Ties marketplace monetization to the partner certification program; certification becomes a commercial gate, not only a quality gate.
**Source:** Slack DM, 2025-07-28.
